
# Future Face-Matching Privacy and Consent Research Note

Static privacy and consent research note for possible future face-matching features.

## Research questions

- What consent is required before face matching?
- How can singers opt in and opt out?
- What data should never be stored?
- What retention policy is required?
- How should false matches be handled?
- What jurisdictions and venues require special review?
- What manual alternatives must remain available?

## Boundary

No face recognition, biometric processing, camera access, photo analysis, identity matching, or data collection is implemented here.
